Channels

Everything the dongle listens to. One dongle covers roughly 1.9 MHz at once; channels outside that range are rejected on save. Squelch is optional per channel (blank = use the default below). PL tone is optional; leave it blank unless you need it.

Receiver

Gain and squelch decide what the dongle hears. Squelch: closer to 0 needs a stronger signal; more negative lets weaker signals through.

Storage and retention

Old recordings are deleted automatically (checked every 10 minutes, no restart needed). Deleting is permanent. Audio is the large part; transcripts are tiny, and keeping them lets you search old calls after their audio is gone. Set 0 to keep forever.
